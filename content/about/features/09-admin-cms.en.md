# Admin CMS

A hidden admin reached by direct URL. Posts, works, and profiles are managed in two languages, and theme, font, and brand settings change instantly. The traffic page shows referral channels, devices, countries, UTM campaigns, and returning IPs. External services like translation, summaries, and TTS report why they failed, providers that keep failing for the same reason are switched off, and this month's usage and the call log are on one screen. Without a database it falls back to static data.
