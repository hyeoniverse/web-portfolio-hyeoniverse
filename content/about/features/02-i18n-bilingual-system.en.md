# i18n Bilingual System

Korean and English switch instantly, and posts, works, and profiles are stored in both languages. Empty language fields are auto-translated by trying DeepL, Google, Gemini, and Claude in the configured order, and re-translated when the source changes. The 🌐 button in the menu switches the whole site into one of 33 languages, and each translation is made once per language and stored.
