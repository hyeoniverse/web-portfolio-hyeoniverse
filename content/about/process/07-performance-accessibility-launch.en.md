# Performance, Accessibility & Launch

Localized admin and public copy into Korean and English, and surfaced silently failing saves as **notifications**. Focus rings, modal focus traps, and AA contrast raised the post editor's Lighthouse Accessibility from **80 to 96**, and the admin post list LCP dropped from **2.1 s to 1.45 s**.
